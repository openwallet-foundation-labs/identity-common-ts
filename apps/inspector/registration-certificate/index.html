Relying party

The access certificate of the relying party, as PEM or base64. The registration certificate has to be for its subject, and the access certificate is checked against ETSI TS 119 411-8 as well.

A JWK, a JWK Set, or certificates as PEM. Without it, the signature is verified with the x5c of the header; give the certificate of the registrar or a trust anchor to check the x5c chain as well.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers share responsibility for the health and direction of the project, including:

- reviewing and merging contributions;
- maintaining the architecture and quality of the monorepo;
- coordinating releases and dependency management;
- triaging issues and supporting contributors;
- maintaining security, CI, and repository hygiene;
- evolving the public APIs and packages in a consistent manner; and
- helping guide the technical roadmap of the project.

The repository-wide `CODEOWNERS` configuration points to the `@openwallet-foundation-labs/identity-common-ts-maintainers` team, reflecting this shared project-wide responsibility.

## Package Ownership

The project currently does not assign exclusive maintainership to individual packages.

As the monorepo grows, maintainers may document primary areas of expertise or responsibility for specific packages. Such assignments should be treated as a routing mechanism for reviews and technical questions rather than exclusive ownership, unless explicitly documented otherwise.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors may be considered for maintainership based on sustained, high-quality contributions to the project, constructive participation in reviews and technical discussions, and demonstrated understanding of the project's architecture and goals.

Changes to the maintainer list should be proposed through a pull request updating this file and agreed upon by the existing maintainers in accordance with the project's governing organization and applicable governance policies.

## Moving to Emeritus Status

A maintainer who is no longer actively participating in the project may move to emeritus status. This should be proposed through a pull request updating this file.

Emeritus maintainers remain recognized for their contributions and may return to active maintainer status through the same project governance process.
